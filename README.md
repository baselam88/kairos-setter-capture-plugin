# Setter Capture Form

Captures the three things that must exist before any sales call books: the problem in the prospect's own words, past attempts + their cost, and the cost of inaction in their number.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

No captures, no call — if a capture is missing the call does not book. Never paraphrase capture 1 into category language; never invent a number for the cost of inaction.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the Setter Capture Form to help me: captures the three things that must exist before any sales call books: the problem in the prospect's own words, past attempts + their cost, and the cost of inaction in their number — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.
